## Findings while checking the PDF arithmetic

* Annex B 'jury use' says *any four guests exceed the 10 t mass margin*. Two four-sets fit: G1+G2+G4+G5 = 7.61 t and G1+G3+G4+G5 = 9.61 t (test `test_annex_claim_any_four_exceed_mass_is_false_for_two_sets`).
* Annex B suggests installed G2, G3, G4 as a Scenario A starting set; it weighs 10.04 t > 10 t, so the governor refuses it as a default (binding: mass).
* Scenario B puts G2, G3, G4 and G5 aboard together (13.07 t, and G3 needs all of Bay 1 while G2 and G5 both need Bay 2 or half of Bay 1). Under A.3/A.4 that set cannot be admitted; B is run as a runtime scenario with admission skipped and mission lines assigned directly (`config/ops.yaml lines_b`).
* PDF B demo: at the genset trip the drone (35 kW) and node (45 kW) are said to force sheds. With the card loads at min 12 (G5 45 + G2 6 + G3 2 + G4 2 in flight = 55 kW) nothing must be shed; the governor logs the declared order and sheds when the load actually rises (rescue: G3's 25 kW charge on top of G4's 35 kW → 111 kW > 100 kW → G5 to low power).
* With G1 holding the only free mast slot, G5's antenna can only go on the hangar roof. If the roof only existed once G4's hangar is fitted, G1 and G5 could never be aboard together without G4 (`hangar_roof.requires_hangar_guest`, default false because A.3 lists the hangar as a ship position).
* Stern ramp (A.3: one craft 7–9 m, ≤ 5 t) is not checked: the G3 card gives only the 5.5 t vehicle+cradle assembly mass, not the vehicle's own mass or length. Recorded as a documented unknown.
* Data links are budgeted at the best end of A.5 (SATCOM 8 Mbit/s, LOS radio 20 Mbit/s). The SATCOM low end (2 Mbit/s) and emission silence (SATCOM and radio at zero) are reported as stress states with every verdict.

## Closing question

**Change one thing: raise the chilled-water loop from 120 kW to 150 kW at 30 °C seawater (+30 kW, i.e. keep the 12 °C rating in warm water).** Across every default set and jury card we ran, the binding class in warm water is cooling or mass; mass is set by the hull, but cooling is equipment. With +30 kW, the G4 charge (35 kW) and the G2 winch (20 kW) can coincide with G5 at full power (135 kW of heat in our 70 kW-charge stress case drops inside the budget), and the cooling-loss stress state (−40 %) still leaves 90 kW — enough for G5 at full power with every window deferred. That removes most G5 throttling and most winch/charge interlocks, which are exactly the decisions a reduced or absent crew would otherwise have to confirm.
